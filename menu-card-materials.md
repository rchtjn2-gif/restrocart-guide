# Menu cards that match your brand: wood, MDF or paper?

A menu is often the first thing a guest holds. Its material says something about price and care before anyone reads a dish name.

## How material shapes perception

- **Solid wood** feels warm and premium. It suits cafes, bistros and bars with a natural or rustic look.
- **MDF** gives a similar look for less, takes engraving and printing well, and keeps a uniform finish.
- **Paper or card** feels light and casual, and is easy to replace.

Hygiene matters too. Wood and MDF with a sealed finish can be wiped between tables; paper menus are usually replaced when they get dirty.

## Wood and MDF: engraving, printing, durability

- **Engraving** lasts longest and suits a logo or a fixed section title.
- **Printed or pasted inserts** on a wooden or MDF board let you change prices without replacing the whole menu.
- Ask for a sealed surface if menus will be wiped often.

## When paper makes sense

- You change dishes or prices often (seasonal menus, new cafes still testing).
- You run promotions or set menus for events.
- You need many copies for a large floor or delivery inserts.
