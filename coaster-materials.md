# Cork, MDF, leatherette or paper: choosing restaurant coasters

Coasters are the cheapest branded object on a restaurant table, and the one guests are most likely to pick up, photograph and take home. Four materials cover almost every use. The right one depends on what you serve and whether you want coasters to last or to leave.

## The quick answer

Cork and thick paper (SBS board) **absorb** condensation. MDF, leatherette and acrylic **do not**; they only catch it. For cold drinks and beer, absorbency matters. For hot drinks and cocktails served up, looks matter more.

## Comparison table

| Material | Best for | Strengths | Watch out for | Typical life |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cork | Cafes, casual dining, cold drinks, warm or natural palettes | Absorbent, pleasant to hold, cheap to die-cut into a logo shape | Stains over time; does not survive soaking | Replace about yearly in busy service |
| MDF | Bars, brands with strong artwork | Sharpest full-colour print; takes foil and spot-UV | Not absorbent; edges must be sealed or they swell | One to two years |
| Leatherette | Premium bars, fine dining | Feels expensive, wipes clean, matches leatherette menu folders and bill holders | Highest cost per piece | Most durable |
| SBS paper | High-volume bars, breweries, events | Very absorbent and cheap; disposable | Consumable by design | Single use or a few uses |

## Decide: keep or give away?

Coasters are not really a durable good. Decide up front whether you are buying something to keep or something guests will take. Breweries and high-volume bars usually do better with paper coasters and stop trying to preserve them. A cafe with a strong look may prefer cork, accepting that some will leave with guests, which, given they carry your logo, is not entirely a loss.

## Shape and finish

- **Round** is standard.
- **Square** reads modern.
- **Hexagonal** reads craft.
- A **die-cut logo shape** shows a brand that pays attention to detail.

Gold-foil and spot-UV finishes are available on MDF and leatherette and photograph well under bar lighting.

## How many to order

- **Reusable materials:** four to six per table, plus a spares box.
- **Paper:** estimate use per cover per month and order quarterly.

Either way, order more than you think. Coasters are the most-lost item on any restaurant table.

## Matching the rest of the table

Coasters look best when they match the menu, bill folder and napkins. If you use leatherette menu folders, leatherette coasters are the natural pair. Wooden or MDF menus pair well with MDF or cork coasters printed with the same artwork.
