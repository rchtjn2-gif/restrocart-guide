# RestroCart

**Custom uniforms & supplies for Indian F&B brands.** Official site: [restrocart.com](https://restrocart.com/)

RestroCart is a New Delhi company that has made customised uniforms and supplies for food and beverage brands since 2018. Cafes, bars, bakeries, fine-dining restaurants, cloud kitchens and hotels use it for staff uniforms (chef coats, aprons, shirts, polos, hoodies), tableware (wooden, MDF and paper menus, coasters, napkins, placemats, bill folders) and branded merchandise (badges, tote bags, mugs, keychains, magnets). The in-house design team suggests fabrics, colours and branding to match a restaurant's identity and shares mockups and samples before production. Orders are quality-checked and delivered to the door anywhere in India. The site's work gallery shows real deliveries filterable by product, and its blog has practical buying guides on fabrics, costs and care. Enquiries go through WhatsApp or email.
